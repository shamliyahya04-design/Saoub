# Saoub — Patent Boundary

## Objective
Separate potentially protectable Saoub mechanisms from third-party technology.

### 1. Open Source / Third Party
Not claimed as Saoub invention.

### 2. Saoub Engineering
Implementation required to realize the system.

### 3. Potential Core Invention
Technical mechanisms requiring novelty, inventive-step, and technical-effect analysis.

## Rule
Patent claims must target the actual inventive mechanism, not merely the product name, model, or collection of tools.
