# Saoub — IP Decisions

## Mandatory Protocol

1. Maintain IP analysis alongside engineering.
2. Research prior art before asserting novelty.
3. Preserve dated evidence of inventions and experiments.
4. Separate Saoub inventions from open-source components.
5. Measure technical effects whenever possible.
6. Record alternatives and limitations.
7. Identify multiple potential inventions.
8. Protect confidential inventive material before public disclosure.
9. Perform IP review before advancing each major phase.
10. Never claim patentability without appropriate patent/legal review.
